Our work

The Foundation writes its success story through the numerous lives saved.

Three programmes across Nigeria: for children who are out of school, older people who need care, and families going through a crisis.

Our flagship programme

Street to School

Getting children who are out of school back into a classroom near home, and keeping them there.

  1. Find

    We find children who are out of school.

  2. Enrol

    We place them in a classroom nearby and cover the uniform, books and fees.

  3. Stay

    We make sure they stay in school.

About Mercy Alexander Foundation

Helping children back into school, and standing with the families around them.

Who we are

Since we began, we have worked with individuals, organisations and government agencies on projects and programmes that have added value to the lives of thousands of Nigerians.

Mercy Alexander Foundation is a Nigerian charity. Our flagship programme, Street to School, launched in 2018, places out-of-school children in classrooms near home and keeps them there.
